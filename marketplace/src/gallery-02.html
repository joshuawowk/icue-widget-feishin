Album art, track info and an idle view

Shows whatever Feishin, or any other client on your server, is playing.

Now playingLarge album art, title, artist, album, and year with duration.
Ambient backdropA blurred copy of the art behind it, an animated EQ badge and a pulsing dot.
Idle viewWhen nothing plays, your eight most recently added albums.
Only My PlaysA switch ignores other users when the server is shared.
Salted-token sign-inSubsonic token authentication, so your password is not sent over the network.
Made to fitArt left in landscape, art on top in portrait, every slot size, your colors and image.
